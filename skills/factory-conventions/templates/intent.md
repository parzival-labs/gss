# Intent: <title>

## Problem

What is wrong or missing today, in the words of the person who raised it.

## Proposed outcome

What is true once this is done. Observable, not a solution.

## Affected users and systems

Who notices the change, and which parts of the codebase or which services are touched.

## Constraints

Deadlines, compatibility, policy, budget, things that must not change.

## Out of scope

What this work item will not do, so the spec does not grow.

## Open questions

Things the originator could not answer. The spec stage must resolve or carry them.
